One joke about office life, written to be read aloud in one breath, judged head to head in pairs. Free to enter.

The entry (141 characters):

> Our manager calls the 4:55 Friday meeting 'a quick sync', and by 6:30 the cleaner is hoovering around our chairs while we just lift our feet.

A manager's euphemism ("a quick sync") collides with a Friday meeting that runs to 6:30, and the punch is the whole room silently lifting its feet for the cleaner — a small, shared defeat everyone in an office recognises.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–200 characters, no trailing newline). Override the output path with `ENTRY_OUTPUT` if the harness needs a different location:

```sh
ENTRY_OUTPUT=/tmp/entry.txt python3 main.py
```
